# Slay the Slang! 🚀

Slay the Slang is an interactive app created for adults to learn Gen Alpha slang. You can learn through flashcards and quizzes, guided to mastery. 

## Features

- **Flashcards**: Learn 10 popular Gen Alpha terms with definitions and applicale examples
- **Quizzes**: Test your knowledge with multiple-choice question quizzes
- **Progress**: Track your learning progress as you master new concepts
- **Mascot**: A bunny companion that reacts to your progress

## Getting Started

To use the app, navigate to the flashcards section first. 

Iterate through the different words and their definitions, more words will be added in the future, but for now stick to these 10 we've provided. 

Simply click the ```flip card``` button to view its definition, click ```next``` or ```back``` if you want to skip or go to a previous word. 

When you are done reviewing definitions, navigate to the home menu and go over to the ```Quiz``` section. This gives you the opportunity for self-paced testing and the ability to continue learning words through application! 

Hands-on learning is the best opportunity to learn, so make sure to take the quizzes to track your progress. 

Our mascot guides all of this learning!
